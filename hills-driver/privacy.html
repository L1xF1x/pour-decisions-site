Hills Driver — Privacy Policy

Developer: CLOGOS · Contact: clogos.games@gmail.com · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

Hills Driver ("the game") is a single-player driving game. We (CLOGOS) do not run our own servers and do not collect, store or sell your personal information. Your progress is stored on your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings (sound, music, vibration, pedal layout, graphics quality, language), your coins, gems, cars, upgrades, paint jobs, unlocked locations, best distances, daily quests, chests and simple statistics locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Advertising (Android version)

The free Android version shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example double coins, a second chance or a faster chest) and occasional full-screen ads between runs, which can be removed with a purchase. Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, approximate location derived from it and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, and wherever the law requires it, we ask for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in the game's settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Purchases (coin and gem packs, the starter pack, ad removal) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; the game only receives a confirmation that the item was bought so it can be granted to you.

Browser version

When the game is played on a web portal (for example CrazyGames or itch.io), the portal may show advertising and process data under its own privacy policy. The game itself stores its progress only in your browser's local storage.

Permissions

  • Vibration — short haptic feedback on landings and crashes; you can turn it off in the settings.
  • Internet — needed only for ads and purchases; the game is fully playable offline.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights and contact

Because we do not hold your personal data, there is nothing for us to export or delete; you can delete local data by uninstalling the game. For questions about ad data, use the controls offered by Google. You can always reach us at clogos.games@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
